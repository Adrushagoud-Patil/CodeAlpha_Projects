# Bank Customer Analysis – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Bank Marketing dataset to understand customer characteristics and identify factors associated with term deposit subscriptions.

## Dataset
- Dataset: UCI Bank Marketing Dataset (`bank-full.csv`)
- Total Records: 45,211
- Original Columns: 17
- Tools: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SciPy, Jupyter Notebook

## Objectives
- Understand the structure of bank customer data.
- Identify missing values and duplicate records.
- Analyze customer demographics and financial information.
- Explore subscription patterns across different customer categories.
- Perform statistical hypothesis testing.
- Present insights through visualizations.

## Analysis Performed
- Data cleaning and inspection
- Descriptive statistics
- Customer subscription analysis
- Job, age, education and marital status analysis
- Housing and personal loan analysis
- Campaign and contact method analysis
- Correlation analysis
- T-test and Chi-square hypothesis testing

## Key Findings
- Overall subscription rate: 11.7%.
- Students and retired customers showed higher subscription rates among job categories.
- Customers without housing loans showed higher subscription rates.
- Previous successful campaign outcomes were associated with higher subscription rates.
- Average call duration was higher for customers who subscribed.
- Statistical tests identified significant differences and associations in the data.

## Conclusion
The analysis identified customer and campaign characteristics associated with term deposit subscriptions. These findings can support further marketing analysis and customer segmentation.
